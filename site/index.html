Tile matching that keeps you sharp.

The classic tile game, with tiles big enough to actually read and a board that meets you where you are. Free to play.

How it plays

Tap a free tile and it moves to your holder. Two that match clear themselves. You have four slots — fill all four without a match and the run is over, so the game is really about which tile you take next, not which two you can see.

Turtle, pyramid, dragon. Boards that get harder as you get better, quietly, without ever making you choose a difficulty.

Made to be read

  • Every tile carries a number and a shape, not just a colour
  • Three text sizes, applied to the whole game
  • A high-contrast theme, and a dark one
  • Colours chosen to stay distinct for colourblind players
  • Fully playable by keyboard, and readable by VoiceOver
