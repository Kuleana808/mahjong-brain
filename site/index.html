Mahjong, with nothing in the way.

Solitaire mahjong for people who want to think, not to be sold to. No ads. No timers. No streaks. No reason to come back tomorrow except that you felt like it.

What it is

The classic tile game — turtle, pyramid, dragon — with large, high-contrast tiles you can actually read, and a coach that teaches you to see the board instead of handing you the answer.

Ask for a hint and it does not point at two tiles. It says: look at the top-right — the two bamboo threes. Taking them releases three more underneath. After a few boards you stop needing to ask.

What it is not

  • No ads
  • No timers
  • No streaks
  • No energy
  • No daily rewards
  • No account

Not in the free version either. There is no free version, in the usual sense — the game is the game, and one payment removes the only thing we ever ask you for.

The board adjusts to you

Quietly. It notices how long you take and how often you ask for help, and picks a board that fits. It will never tell you it did that, and there is no difficulty setting to get wrong.
