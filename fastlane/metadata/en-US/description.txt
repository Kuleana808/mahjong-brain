Mahjong Brain is a tile-matching game built around clarity, comfort, and satisfying play.

Choose matching free tiles and keep an eye on the four-slot holder. Matching pairs clear automatically. If four unmatched tiles fill the holder, the round ends—so each move matters.

• Large, readable mahjong tiles
• Layered boards designed for phones and iPad
• Helpful hints, shuffle, and undo controls
• A short interactive tutorial
• Adjustable text, motion, sound, vibration, and contrast settings
• Progress saved on your device

Mahjong Brain uses original artwork and can be played without creating an account. Optional Sign in with Apple and purchase features only appear when their secure services are configured.
